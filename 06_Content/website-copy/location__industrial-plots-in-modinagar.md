## The short answer

Isapur Industrial Park is 11.4 km by road from Modinagar bus stand, about 28 minutes. It is on the Hapur-Modinagar Road at Yusufpur Isapur. Plots start from 300 sq yards.

## Why factory owners in Modinagar look here

- **You stay on a road you know.** The Hapur-Modinagar Road connects Modinagar with Hapur through Sarawa, Yusufpur Isapur and Bhojpur. Your staff, suppliers and transporters already use it.
- **Straight-line, Modinagar bus stand is 9.3 km away.** By road it is 11.4 km. We always give the road figure first because trucks drive roads, not lines.
- **The expressway is close.** Delhi-Meerut Expressway on-ramp at Bhojpur is 4.7 km by road, about 8 minutes. That is the quick way towards Ghaziabad and Delhi.
