## The short answer

Isapur Industrial Park has 50 ft RCC roads, street lighting and planted greenery. These are the three things you can see and check when you visit.

## 50 ft RCC roads

RCC means reinforced cement concrete. A concrete road holds up under loaded trucks better than a thin bitumen road, and it does not soften in summer heat. A 50 ft road gives room for a truck to turn and for two vehicles to pass. That matters when you load and unload every day.

What to check on your visit: the road surface, the edges, the turning space at the plot corner. [OWNER TO CONFIRM: road widths inside the park, which roads are 50 ft, which are narrower]

## Street lighting

Lit roads help night shifts, late loading and safe movement of people. Street lights also make the park easier to find after dark.

What to check on your visit: how many poles, how far apart, and whether they are lit at dusk. [OWNER TO CONFIRM: number of street lights installed and working]
