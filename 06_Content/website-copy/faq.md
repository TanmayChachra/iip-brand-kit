## Roads, lights and site

### What are the roads like?
The park has 50 ft RCC roads. RCC is concrete, which stands up to loaded trucks better than a thin bitumen layer. A 50 ft width gives room to turn and for vehicles to pass. Check the surface and the turning space at your plot corner on your visit.

### Can trucks reach the plots?
Yes, the park has 50 ft RCC roads, which are built for heavy vehicles. Still, check the turn into your own plot on a visit, using the size of vehicle you actually run. Bring a driver if you can, because they judge turning space best. [OWNER TO CONFIRM: roads inside the park and their widths]

### Is there street lighting?
Yes, the park has street lighting along its roads, which helps night shifts and late loading. On your visit, look at the poles, the spacing and whether they switch on at dusk. Ask how many are installed and who looks after them. [OWNER TO CONFIRM: number of lights installed and working]

### Is there greenery in the park?
Yes, the park has planted greenery along its roads. It gives shade, helps with dust a little and makes the place better for workers and visitors. Ask on your visit what is planted, how it is watered and what more is planned. [OWNER TO CONFIRM: what is planted and where]

### What about power, water and drainage?
These details are still to be confirmed by the owner, so do not rely on a web page for them. Ask for written details on the call: power load and who supplies it, water source and supply hours, and where waste water goes. Then see each one on your site visit. ## Location

### Where is Isapur Industrial Park?
It is at Yusufpur Isapur on the Hapur-Modinagar Road, near Bhojpur, in Ghaziabad district, UP 201022. It is 4.7 km by road, about 8 minutes, from the Delhi-Meerut Expressway on-ramp at Bhojpur (Google Maps, 6 November 2026). See the [location page](/location/).

### How far is the Delhi-Meerut Expressway?
The on-ramp at Bhojpur is 4.7 km by road, about 8 minutes. In a straight line the nearest expressway point is about 3.9 km and the Bhojpur exit about 4.3 km. We give the road figure first because trucks drive roads.

### How far is Modinagar?
Modinagar bus stand is 11.4 km by road, about 28 minutes, from the park. In a straight line it is about 9.3 km. Times come from Google Maps on 6 November 2026 with live traffic and will change by hour and day, so do your own test drive before you decide.

### How far is Hapur?
Hapur bus stand is 13.4 km by road, about 31 minutes, from the park. In a straight line it is about 13.0 km. The Hapur-Modinagar Road connects the two towns and passes the park. Times come from Google Maps on 6 November 2026 and vary with traffic, so test drive it.

### How far is Meerut, Ghaziabad or Delhi?
By road via the expressway: Meerut City railway station 29.3 km (about 56 min), Ghaziabad railway station 34.7 km (about 47 min), Anand Vihar ISBT in Delhi 46.0 km (about 89 min). Tolls apply and times change with traffic.
