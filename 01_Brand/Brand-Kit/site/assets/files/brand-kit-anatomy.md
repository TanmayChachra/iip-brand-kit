VERDICT: A complete brand kit has 15 to 17 sections; IIP already has logo, type, icons, graphic elements and applications, and is missing story, voice, social, digital, motion and governance.
EVIDENCE: Sources agree on core logo, colour, type, imagery, voice, do and do not; fuller guides add story, layout, applications, governance (reported, from search result summaries, pages not fully read).
CAVEAT: Frontify, Canva, Adobe, Figma and Brandfolder pages did not appear in results, so their templates are not cited; no published industrial park brand manual with hoarding specs was found.
