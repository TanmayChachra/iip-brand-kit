## What we collect

- **Enquiry form.** Your name, phone number and any details you type. We use them only to call you back about plots at Isapur Industrial Park.
- **Calls and WhatsApp.** When you call or message us we see your number and what you tell us.
- **Website analytics.** We measure which pages are read and which buttons are pressed, so we can improve the site. We use Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity through Google Tag Manager. Clarity masks form inputs. Analytics does not collect your name or phone number.

## What we do not do

We do not sell your details. We do not send your enquiry to other companies.
