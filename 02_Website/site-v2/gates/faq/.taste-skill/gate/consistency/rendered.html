Plots and price

What plot sizes do you have?

Plots start from 300 sq yards, about 2,700 sq ft. Call us for the sizes open today.

What is the price of a plot?

Contact for price. Rates depend on size, position and payment plan. Call us and we share today's list.

Can I pay for a plot in instalments?

Ask us about payment plans on the call. Get any plan in writing, with dates and amounts, before you book.

How do I buy an industrial plot here?

Call, visit the site, pick a plot on the layout plan, read the papers with your own advisor, then book and register.

Plot paperwork

Which documents should I check before buying a plot?

Check land use, title and sale papers, the layout plan, the plot number on it, and any dues. Ask your own lawyer to read them.

Can I use the plot for a factory or a warehouse?

Plots are meant for industrial use. Before you pay, confirm in the papers that your exact activity is allowed on that land.

What about power, water and drainage for a plot?

We do not publish these details here. Ask for them in writing on the call, then see each one on your visit.

Do you share the plot list on WhatsApp?

Message us on WhatsApp with your name, your industry and the plot size you need. We reply with what we can share.

Plot roads and lights

What are the roads inside the industrial park like?

The park has 50 ft RCC roads. RCC is concrete, which carries loaded trucks better than a thin bitumen layer.

Can trucks reach the plots?

Yes, the 50 ft RCC roads are built for heavy vehicles. On your visit, check the turn into your plot with your own vehicle.

Is there street lighting in the industrial park?

Yes, the park has street lighting along its roads. Look at the poles and the spacing on your visit.

Is there greenery in the industrial park?

Yes, the park has planted greenery along its roads. It gives shade and makes the place better for workers.

Location and distances

Where is Isapur Industrial Park?

At Yusufpur Isapur on the Hapur-Modinagar Road, near Bhojpur, Ghaziabad, UP 201022. See the location page.

How far is the expressway from the plots?

The Bhojpur on-ramp is 4.7 km by road, about 8 minutes. In a straight line the nearest expressway point is about 3.9 km.

How far are Modinagar and Hapur from the plots?

Modinagar bus stand is 11.4 km by road, about 28 minutes. Hapur bus stand is 13.4 km, about 31 minutes. Test drive it, traffic changes.

How far are Meerut, Ghaziabad and Delhi from the plots?

By road via the expressway: Meerut City station 29.3 km, Ghaziabad station 34.7 km, Anand Vihar ISBT 46.0 km. Tolls apply.

Visit the plots

Can I visit the plots before I decide?

Yes. Call us and we fix a time and meet you at the gate. Open 8 am to 8 pm every day except Tuesday.

What should I ask on a site visit?

Ask to see the plot on the layout plan, the road in front of it, the lights and the papers. Our buying checklist lists more.

How do I contact you?

Call us, message us on WhatsApp, or use the form on the contact page. We call back.

Who are the industrial plots for?

Local factory owners and small industrial buyers: a first workshop, a move out of a rented shed, or a growing storage unit.
