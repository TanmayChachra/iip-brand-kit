What you get on site: roads, lights and greenery

Isapur Industrial Park has 50 ft RCC roads, street lighting and planted greenery.

Book a site visit

50 ft RCC roads

RCC means reinforced cement concrete. A concrete road holds up under loaded trucks better than a thin bitumen road, and it does not soften in summer heat. A 50 ft road gives room for a truck to turn and for two vehicles to pass. That matters when you load and unload every day.

What to check on your visit: the road surface, the edges, the turning space at the plot corner.

Street lighting at the plots

Lit roads help night shifts, late loading and safe movement of people. Street lights also make the park easier to find after dark.

What to check on your visit: how many poles, how far apart, and whether they are lit at dusk.

Planted greenery near the plots

Trees and green strips along the park give shade, cut dust a little and make the place pleasant for workers and visitors.

What this page does not claim about plots

We list only what you can see on site. Other facilities such as drainage, power load, water and security are not listed here until we have confirmed them. Ask us on the call, and check each one on your visit.
