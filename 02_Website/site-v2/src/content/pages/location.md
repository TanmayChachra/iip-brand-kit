## Find the park

Hapur-Modinagar Road, Yusufpur Isapur, Ghaziabad, UP 201022. Look for the gate and the petrol pump at the entry and exit.

[Get directions](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Isapur+Industrial+Park+Hapur+Modinagar+Road)

## Distances by road

| From the park | By road | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Delhi-Meerut Expressway on-ramp (Bhojpur) | 4.2 km | about 5 min |
| Modinagar centre | 10.7 km | about 14 min |
| Hapur centre | 13.6 km | about 15 min |
| Meerut centre | 29.9 km | about 29 min |
| Eastern Peripheral Expressway junction (Kallu Garhi) | 17.8 km | about 15 min |
| Ghaziabad railway station | 33.5 km | about 30 min |
| Anand Vihar, Delhi | 46 km | about 40 min |
| CONCOR container depot at Dadri | 46.3 km | about 45 min |

Driving distances from the park, measured on the road, no traffic (13 November 2026). Meerut, Ghaziabad and Delhi are by the expressway.

## The road to the plots

The Hapur-Modinagar Road runs through Sarawa, Yusufpur Isapur and Bhojpur. Leave the expressway at Bhojpur and the park is on the road ahead.
