## Plots address and distances



Address: Hapur-Modinagar Road, Yusufpur Isapur, Ghaziabad, UP 201022.

## Distances by road

| From the park | By road | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Delhi-Meerut Expressway on-ramp (Bhojpur) | 4.7 km | about 8 min |
| Modinagar bus stand | 11.4 km | about 28 min |
| Hapur bus stand | 13.4 km | about 31 min |
| Meerut City railway station | 29.3 km | about 56 min (expressway, toll) |
| Ghaziabad railway station | 34.7 km | about 47 min (expressway, toll) |
| Anand Vihar ISBT, Delhi | 46.0 km | about 89 min (expressway, toll) |

How we measured: Google Maps, driving mode, 6 November 2026 at about 6:50 pm with live traffic, from a point at the park. Times change with traffic and tolls. Plan on a test drive.

## Straight-line distances

Straight-line figures are shorter than a drive. The nearest point of the expressway is about 3.9 km straight-line, and the Bhojpur exit is about 4.3 km straight-line. Modinagar bus stand is about 9.3 km and Hapur bus stand about 13.0 km straight-line. We always say which kind of distance we mean.

## The road to the plots

The Hapur-Modinagar Road runs through Sarawa, Yusufpur Isapur and Bhojpur. The park sits on this road. From the expressway you leave at Bhojpur and drive to the park.
