## What an approval means

An industrial park starts as a plan. The plan shows the roads, the plots, the green strips and the number of every plot. Before the plots go on sale, the plan goes to the local authority. Here that is Zila Panchayat Ghaziabad.

The authority checks the plan and approves it. In plain words: the authority has passed the plan of roads and plots, and the park is laid out to it.

Isapur Industrial Park is approved by Zila Panchayat Ghaziabad, all three phases. Our [approvals page](/approvals/) lists what we hold.

## Ask for the papers themselves

Ask the seller for the approval papers themselves, not a photograph of a brochure. We lay them on the table at your visit, with any letter that came with them, for the plot you like.

## Six things to look for

1. **The authority.** The paper should name Zila Panchayat Ghaziabad.
2. **A reference number.** Write it down.
3. **A date.** Note it. The approval date and the drawing date are two different dates, and both should be there.
4. **Signatures.** The paper should carry the signatures of the officers of the authority.
5. **The title block.** It gives the name of the park, the village, the tehsil and the district. Check that they match the land you are visiting.
6. **Your plot number.** Find your plot on the drawing. Read its size, its road and its neighbours.

## Match the drawing to the ground

A drawing is only useful if you can stand on it. Walk the park with it in your hand.

- Find the road in front of your plot. The drawing gives its width. The main roads are 50 ft wide, so stand in the middle and see how it looks with a loaded truck.
- Count the plots between yours and the corner. The numbers should run in the same order on the ground and on the paper.
- Look at the green strips and the divider on the drawing, then look for the trees on the ground.
- Check that the plot you are shown is the plot on your sale papers. The number and the size must be the same in both places.

## Match the drawing to your papers

Your registry papers name your plot by number and size. Put them beside the approved plan. The plot number, the area and the park name should read the same on both. If one line differs, ask why before you sign.

Your advisor will want to run this check, so bring them along. A CA or a lawyer who knows land papers can read the approval, the letter and the registry draft in one sitting.

## Building approval comes later

The park approval covers the roads and plots. When you build, you take building approval for your own shed. Plan for it when you plan your drawings and your budget.
