## What approved by Zila Panchayat means

Zila Panchayat Ghaziabad is the local authority. It checks the plan of roads and plots for a park and gives its approval. Isapur Industrial Park is approved by Zila Panchayat Ghaziabad, all three phases. You buy a plot in a park the local authority has passed.

## What it means for you

- The roads, the plots and the green strips follow an approved plan.
- Your plot number sits on that plan, so you know exactly what you are buying.
- You can plan your shed with confidence. Each plot owner takes building approval before construction.

## See the papers

Ask us to show you the approval for the plot you like. Bring your advisor. We show every paper.
