## Who a shop in the park serves

A factory park has a working day with a shape. Shifts start, trucks roll in, and drivers wait to load. Between all this, people need tea, lunch, a repair or a packet of biscuits.

Your customers are the people who work here and the people who visit. Factory staff. Truck drivers. Buyers on a site visit. A shop gives all of them a place to stop without leaving the park.

## Any trade you like

Every shop in the park is commercial, and the choice of business is yours. We do not fix a list of trades for you. Three trades come to mind first:

- **A confectionery.** Sweets, snacks and tea for the shift change.
- **Quick food.** A counter that serves a hot plate fast to people on a break.
- **Tyre repair.** Trucks run on tyres. A tyre repair shop at the park entry serves every driver who comes in or goes out.

Your trade may be a stationery counter, a mobile repair desk or a tea stall. If you serve factory people, talk to us.

## Where the shops sit

Shops are the small commercial plots in the park. You can see them on the [plot maps](/plot-maps/), where each one is marked as a shop. The park also has a petrol pump at the entry and exit, so the entry is a place where people already stop.

The roads inside are 50 ft RCC, lit at night and lined with planted trees. A customer walks to your shop on a road built for trucks.

## Who should look at a shop

- A trader who already serves factories on the Hapur-Modinagar Road and wants a fixed address.
- A family business that wants a second counter close to a working workforce.
- A factory owner who wants a shop beside the plot.
- An investor who wants a commercial plot in an industrial park.

Start with a visit. We show you what is available, the layout of the park and the papers. We get to know you and your business, because trust matters on both sides. Then the same steps as for any plot apply. See [how buying works](/how-buying-works/) for them.

Bring your advisor. We show every paper, and the [layout approval guide](/layout-approvals/) tells you what to look for.
