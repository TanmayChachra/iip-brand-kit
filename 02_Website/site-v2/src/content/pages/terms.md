## What this site is

A guide to Isapur Industrial Park. It is not an offer, a booking or a contract. A plot is sold only through our written papers, after you have seen the plot and the documents.

## Distances

Distances are by road unless the page says straight-line. They come from Google Maps on the date shown on the page. Traffic and road works change the time. Check the route on the day you travel.

## Drawings and plot sizes

Maps, layout drawings and illustrations show the park's plan. They are not to scale unless marked. Plot numbers, sizes and positions are final only in the sanctioned layout plan we give you with the papers. Photographs of the surroundings are for context. Some are stock photographs, credited in the footer.

## Facts and approvals

We state a fact on this site only when we hold the paper that supports it. Ask us to show you any document. The approvals pages explain what each paper covers.

## Using the site

You may read, print and share pages for your own business use. Do not copy the site, the logo or the drawings for another business. The name and logo belong to Isapur Industrial Park.

## Links and other sites

Links to maps and other sites are for your convenience. We do not control those sites.

## Changes and law

We may change the site or these terms at any time. The law of India applies. Courts at Ghaziabad can hear any dispute.
