## A factory on a 100 sq yard plot, waiting for a visit

Isapur Industrial Park has a factory on a 100 sq yard plot for rent, and it is available now. Call to see it. The park also has other industrial sheds and factories for rent or lease, and plots for sale. Ask what is free today.

100 sq yards is about 900 sq ft of plot. That is a compact unit for a team that wants to start working this month.

## Who a rented factory suits

- **Start-ups.** Start production in a real factory without buying land first.
- **Small manufacturers.** A workshop of your own, close to your suppliers and buyers on the Hapur-Modinagar Road.
- **A trial run before buying.** Work in the park, learn how your trucks and staff move, then choose a plot with confidence.
- **Expansion space.** Add a second unit without disturbing the first.
- **Warehouses.** A shed for stock, close to the Delhi-Meerut Expressway.

## Why rent inside Isapur Industrial Park

- **The expressway is 4.2 km by road.** Your trucks reach the Delhi-Meerut Expressway entry in about 5 minutes. See [roads and freight](/roads-and-freight/).
- **50 ft RCC roads.** Wide, hard roads made for loaded trucks.
- **Street lights and planted trees.** The park is lit at night and green by day.
- **Power 24/7.** Government lines come in from both the Modinagar side and the Hapur side.
- **An IndianOil pump at the entry and exit.** Your trucks fuel up at the gate.
- **Approved by Zila Panchayat Ghaziabad.** You work in a park the local authority has passed. See the [approvals](/approvals/).

## Rent or lease, in two lines

**Rent** is a monthly rent agreement, usually for a short or medium term.

**A lease** runs longer and is written in a registered lease deed.

Bring your advisor and choose the one that fits your plan.

## Rent now, buy later

Many owners like to start on rent and buy when the business has grown. When that day comes, the park has plots from 300 sq yards. See [industrial plots for sale](/industrial-plots/) and [how buying works](/how-buying-works/).
