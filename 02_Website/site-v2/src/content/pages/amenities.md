## 50 ft RCC roads

RCC means reinforced cement concrete. A concrete road holds up under loaded trucks. At 50 ft wide, a truck can turn and two vehicles can pass, which helps when you load and unload every day. On your visit, look at the road surface and the turn at your plot corner.

## Street lighting

Lights run along the roads. Night shifts and late loading get a lit road, and the park is easy to find after dark. Come at dusk and see them come on.

## Planted greenery

Trees are planted along the park. They give shade to workers and visitors.

## Power 24/7

Power runs 24/7 on government lines, coming in from both the Modinagar side and the Hapur side.

## A petrol pump at the gate

A petrol pump sits at the park entry and exit. Your trucks fill up on the way in and on the way out.

## Shops inside the park

Small commercial plots serve the people who work here. A sweet shop, a quick-food counter, a tyre repair stall.
