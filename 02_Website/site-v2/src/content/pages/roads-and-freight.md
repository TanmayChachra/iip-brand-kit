## Out to the expressway

The park sits on the [Hapur-Modinagar Road](/location/) at Yusufpur Isapur. Your loaded truck turns out of the gate and reaches the Delhi-Meerut Expressway entry at Bhojpur in 4.2 km.

From there the expressway opens the way to Ghaziabad and Delhi. Anand Vihar in Delhi is 46 km by road from the park.

## Modinagar and Hapur, your two market towns

Modinagar is 10.7 km away and Hapur is 13.6 km. Your suppliers, transporters and staff already use this road. Serve either town and you are never far from your market.

See the town pages for [Modinagar](/location/industrial-plots-in-modinagar/) and [Hapur](/location/industrial-plots-in-hapur/).

## Round the ring road

About 17.8 km from the park the Eastern Peripheral Expressway meets the Delhi-Meerut Expressway. The Eastern Peripheral Expressway is the ring road that lets a truck go around Delhi instead of through it. Your driver picks the route that suits the load.

## Meerut and the north

Meerut is 29.9 km by road. It is the next big city up the expressway, with its own market and its own buyers waiting.

## Shipping a container

Some orders go overseas. The container depot at Dadri, run by CONCOR, is the rail container terminal for this part of the country, 46.3 km by road from the park.

The flow is simple. Your truck carries the container from the park to the depot at Dadri. From there it goes by rail to the ports. The park is not on that rail line, so your truck runs the first leg. Your shipping agent fixes the dates.

## Bring your own truck

Drive the 50 ft RCC road, take the turn into your plot and fill up at the petrol pump at the entry and exit. Then drive to the expressway entry and time it yourself.
