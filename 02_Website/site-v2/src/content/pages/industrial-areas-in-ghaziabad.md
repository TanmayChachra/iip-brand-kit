## Where the belts are

Ghaziabad district has older industrial areas close to the city, such as Sahibabad and Loni. Newer plotted parks have come up along the Meerut Road corridor and along Hapur Road toward Modinagar. Each belt has a different land price, road standard and distance to the Delhi-Meerut Expressway.

## What to compare

Put the same five questions to every park and write down the answers.

**Road width.** Can a loaded truck turn inside the park?
**Distance to the expressway.** Ask for the road distance, not a straight line.
**Plot size.** Does the smallest plot fit your unit and your growth?
**Papers.** Ask to see the layout map and read every paper with your own advisor.
**Visit.** Go on a working day and look at the roads, the lights and the units already running.

## Where Hapur Road fits

Isapur Industrial Park is on Hapur-Modinagar Road at Yusufpur Isapur, in Ghaziabad district. The road distance to the Delhi-Meerut Expressway on-ramp at Bhojpur is 4.7 km, about 8 minutes (Google Maps, 6 November 2026, times vary with traffic). Plots start from 300 sq yards. Roads are 50 ft wide RCC, with street lighting and planted greenery.
