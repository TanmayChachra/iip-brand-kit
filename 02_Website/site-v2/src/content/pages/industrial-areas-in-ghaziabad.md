## Where the belts are

Ghaziabad district has older industrial areas close to the city, such as Sahibabad and Loni. Newer plotted parks have come up along the Meerut Road corridor and along Hapur Road toward Modinagar. Each belt has its own road standard and its own distance to the Delhi-Meerut Expressway.

## What to compare

Ask every park the same five questions and write down the answers.

- **Road width.** Can a loaded truck turn inside the park?
- **Distance to the expressway.** Ask for the road distance.
- **Plot size.** Does the smallest plot fit your unit and your growth?
- **Papers.** Ask to see the approval papers. Bring your own advisor.
- **Visit.** Go on a working day and look at the roads, the lights and the units already running.

## Where Hapur Road fits

Isapur Industrial Park is on Hapur-Modinagar Road at Yusufpur Isapur, in Ghaziabad district. The Delhi-Meerut Expressway on-ramp at Bhojpur is 4.2 km by road, about 5 minutes. Plots run from 300 sq yards, on 50 ft RCC roads with street lighting and planted greenery.
