## Who we are

Isapur Industrial Park, Hapur-Modinagar Road, Yusufpur Isapur, Ghaziabad, UP 201022. We decide why and how your details are used.

## What we collect

- **Site visit form.** Your name, phone number, plot size and the day you can visit. The form opens WhatsApp with these details typed in. They reach us only when you press send in WhatsApp.
- **Calls and WhatsApp.** When you call or message us we see your number and what you tell us. Tapping a call or WhatsApp link on this site needs no cookie choice.
- **Page counts, no cookies.** Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views, country, device type and the page you came from. It sets no cookie and does not follow you from visit to visit, so it runs without a choice.
- **Detailed analytics, only if you accept.** Pages read, buttons pressed, device type, the city you are in and how you reached the site. We use Google Analytics through Google Tag Manager. We may also use Microsoft Clarity, which records anonymous screen activity such as clicks and scrolls. Form fields are masked in Clarity, so your name and phone number are not recorded. These tools set cookies on your device.
- **Basic server records.** Our host, Cloudflare, sees your IP address and browser details when a page loads. This is how any website works.

## Why we use your details

- To call you back and arrange a site visit. You gave us the details for this.
- To see which pages help visitors and fix the ones that do not. This runs only after you accept analytics.

We do not sell your details. We do not send your enquiry to other companies. We do not send marketing to your number unless you ask for it.

## Cookies

<span id="cookies"></span>The site sets no cookie until you choose, and the page counts above use none. A box at the bottom of the screen asks you to Accept or Reject analytics. Both buttons look the same and do what they say.

- **Accept:** Google Analytics loads and sets its cookies. Microsoft Clarity loads too, if we have switched it on.
- **Reject:** no analytics tool loads. The site works the same.
- Your choice is kept on your device for 6 months, then we ask again.
- You can change your mind at any time with **Cookie settings** in the footer of every page. Choosing Reject there stops analytics and clears its cookies from this site.

## Who else handles your details

- **Google** (Analytics and Tag Manager), only after you accept.
- **Microsoft** (Clarity), only after you accept and only if switched on.
- **Cloudflare**, which hosts the site.
- **WhatsApp (Meta)**, if you send us a message there.

These companies may handle data on servers outside India. They have their own privacy terms.

## How long we keep it

- Enquiry details: for 12 months after our last contact with you, then we delete them.
- Analytics data: 14 months, then Google deletes it.

## Your rights

You can ask us to show you the details we hold, correct them, delete them, or stop using them. You can take back a consent you gave, as easily as you gave it. Write to us at the address below or call. You can also name a person to act for you. If you are under 18, please do not use the form. A parent or guardian can call us instead.

## Questions and complaints

Write to our grievance contact at sales@isapurindustrialpark.com. We reply within 30 days. If you are not happy with our answer you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India once it is open for complaints.
