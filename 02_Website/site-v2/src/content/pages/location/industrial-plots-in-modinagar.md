## Why factory owners in Modinagar look here

- **You stay on a road you know.** The Hapur-Modinagar Road connects Modinagar with Hapur through Sarawa, Yusufpur Isapur and Bhojpur. Your staff, suppliers and transporters already use it.
- **The expressway is close.** The Delhi-Meerut Expressway on-ramp at Bhojpur is 4.2 km by road, about 5 minutes. That is the quick way towards Ghaziabad and Delhi.

## Inside the park

50 ft RCC roads, street lighting, planted greenery and power 24/7. A petrol pump sits at the entry and exit. See [roads and lighting](/amenities/).
