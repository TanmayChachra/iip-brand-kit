## Ghaziabad district, outer side

Ghaziabad is a large district and the city is dense and busy. This park is on the outer side, on the Hapur-Modinagar Road near Bhojpur, close to the Delhi-Meerut Expressway. Ghaziabad railway station is 33.5 km by road, about 30 minutes, by the expressway.

## Inside the park

50 ft RCC roads, street lighting, planted greenery and power 24/7. A petrol pump sits at the entry and exit. See [roads and lighting](/amenities/).

## Plots

From 300 sq yards, about 2,700 sq ft. See [plot sizes and how buying works](/industrial-plots/) and the [plot maps](/plot-maps/).
