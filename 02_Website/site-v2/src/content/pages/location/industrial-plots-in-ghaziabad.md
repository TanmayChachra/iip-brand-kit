## What "Ghaziabad" means here

Ghaziabad is a large district, and the city itself is dense and busy. This park is on the outer side, on the Hapur-Modinagar Road near Bhojpur, close to the Delhi-Meerut Expressway. If you want plot space that is easier to find than city land, this belt is worth a look.

## Plot distances to know
