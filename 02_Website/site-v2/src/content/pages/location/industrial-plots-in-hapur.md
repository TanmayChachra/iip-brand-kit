## For factory owners based in Hapur

- **Same road, one straight drive.** From Hapur you take the Hapur-Modinagar Road through Sarawa to Yusufpur Isapur. The park is on that road.
- **Straight-line, Hapur bus stand is 13.0 km away.** The road figure is 13.4 km, so the road runs almost straight.
- **Expressway when you need it.** The Delhi-Meerut Expressway on-ramp at Bhojpur is 4.7 km by road from the park, about 8 minutes.
