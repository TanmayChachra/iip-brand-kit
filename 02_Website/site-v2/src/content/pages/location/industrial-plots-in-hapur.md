## For factory owners based in Hapur

- **Same road, one straight drive.** From Hapur you take the Hapur-Modinagar Road through Sarawa to Yusufpur Isapur. The park is on that road.
- **The expressway when you need it.** The Delhi-Meerut Expressway on-ramp at Bhojpur is 4.2 km by road from the park, about 5 minutes.

## Inside the park

50 ft RCC roads, street lighting, planted greenery and power 24/7. A petrol pump sits at the entry and exit. See [roads and lighting](/amenities/).

## Plots

From 300 sq yards, about 2,700 sq ft. See [plot sizes and how buying works](/industrial-plots/) and the [plot maps](/plot-maps/).

## Visit from Hapur

Plan about half an hour each way and come and see the plots. See [all locations](/location/) and the [questions and answers](/faq/).
