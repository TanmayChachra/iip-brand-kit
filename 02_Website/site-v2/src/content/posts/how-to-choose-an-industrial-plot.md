## The short answer on choosing a plot

Work backwards from your trucks and your next five years. Fix the plot size, check the road in front of it, time the drive to a highway, get power and drainage in writing, read the papers with your own advisor, and visit twice before you pay.

## Steps 1 to 3: size, road and highway

- **Size.** List your machines, the clear space around them and a loading side where a truck can turn. A 300 sq yard plot is about 2,700 sq ft, roughly 50 ft by 54 ft: a small workshop, light assembly or storage. Add room to grow.
- **Road.** Check width, surface and the corner turn into the plot with your biggest vehicle. Concrete (RCC) holds loaded trucks better than a thin bitumen layer. Isapur Industrial Park has 50 ft RCC roads.
- **Highway.** Ask for distance by road, not straight line, and drive it at the hour your trucks run. From the park, the Delhi-Meerut Expressway on-ramp at Bhojpur is 4.7 km by road, about 8 minutes (Google Maps, 6 November 2026).

## Steps 4 and 5: utilities and papers

- **Power, water, drainage.** Do not accept "available". Ask for the load and supplier, the water source and hours, and where waste water goes, all in writing. A park that cannot answer is a risk to price in.
- **Papers.** This is a general guide, not legal advice. Have your own lawyer read the land use, the title, the sanctioned layout plan with your plot number, and any dues.

## Steps 6 and 7: total cost and visits

- **Cost.** Add registry and stamp duty, any maintenance charges, the power connection, a boundary wall and shed, drainage inside the plot, and shifting your machines. A cheaper plot can end up dearer.
- **Visits.** Go once by day with questions and once at another hour to watch the road work. Take photos and stand on the plot.
