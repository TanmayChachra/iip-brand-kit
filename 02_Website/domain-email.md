**Verdict:** Buy `isapurindustrialpark.in` as the main domain, and also buy `isapurindustrialpark.com` and point it to the main one with a 301 redirect. All 116 names I checked (29 names x .com, .in, .co.in, .org) are unregistered today, so you do not need to hurry or compromise. For email, start free with Zoho Mail Forever Free (a real `sales@` mailbox, web only) and move to Google Workspace Business Base (₹99 per user per month) when sales volume grows. Cloudflare Email Routing is a free fallback that only receives and forwards.
**Evidence:** registry checks below (V). Prices come from one official registrar page plus price trackers (R), so confirm at checkout.
**Caveat:** "Available" is a registry answer at one moment. A domain can be bought by someone else before you pay. Prices change often. I bought nothing and created no accounts.

Labels: V = verified from a registry or an official page on 2026-11-08, R = reported by a third party, G = guess.

## 1. How availability was checked (V)
- `.com` and `.org`: official RDAP servers (Verisign for .com, Public Interest Registry for .org). HTTP 404 means "no such domain". I confirmed the checker works: `google.com` returned 200 with its lock statuses.

It replied "is available for registration" for every candidate and returned full records for registered controls (`flipkart.in`, `tata.co.in`). The .IN RDAP service rate-limited me (HTTP 429), so whois was used instead.
- A DNS check (Google public DNS) also returned "no such domain" for every candidate. No candidate has a website.
- Full table: `domain-result.json` (116 rows).

## 3. What to buy
- **Primary:** `isapurindustrialpark.in`.
- **Protect:** `isapurindustrialpark.com` (redirect to the primary). Optional later: `isapurpark.in` for print and QR.
- **Do not buy:** hyphen names, numbers, `.org`, and the many long variations. One strong domain is better for SEO than ten.
- **Privacy protection:** free at Namecheap and Cloudflare for .com (R). Hostinger and others vary, check at checkout. Note: Indian registries mask owner details by default for .in in many cases, but confirm on the registrar page (G).
- **KYC:** one source reports that `.in` registrations need e-KYC since December 2025 (R, https://cybernews.com/best-domain-registrars/best-india-domain-registrars/). Keep your PAN or Aadhaar details ready, buy during working hours.
- **Who to buy from:** the registrar for `.in` must support `.in`. Cloudflare Registrar does not list `.in` on its TLD policy page (V, https://www.cloudflare.com/tld-policies/ , checked today), so buy `.in` from an Indian-friendly registrar (Hostinger, GoDaddy India, BigRock, Namecheap) and still use Cloudflare for DNS and hosting by changing the nameservers. Buy `.com` at the same place for one bill, or at Cloudflare at cost.

## 7. DNS records (generic, no secrets)
Replace `yourdomain.in` with the final name. Use the exact values shown in each service's setup screen, they win over this list. Records marked G come from general knowledge and must be confirmed in the dashboard.

**Zoho Mail (India data centre values, G):**
| Type | Name | Value | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| MX | @ | mx.zoho.in | 10 |
| MX | @ | mx2.zoho.in | 20 |
| MX | @ | mx3.zoho.in | 50 |
| TXT (SPF) | @ | `v=spf1 include:zoho.in ~all` | |
| TXT (DKIM) | `zmail._domainkey` | the long key Zoho shows after you add the domain | |
| TXT (verify) | @ | the code Zoho shows | |
If Zoho says your account is on the `.com` data centre use `mx.zoho.com`, `mx2.zoho.com`, `mx3.zoho.com` and `include:zoho.com`.

**Cloudflare Email Routing:** turn it on in Cloudflare, it adds its own MX records (`route1.mx.cloudflare.net`, `route2.mx.cloudflare.net`, `route3.mx.cloudflare.net`) and an SPF record automatically (G). Do not run Zoho and Email Routing on the same address at once (the MX records conflict).

**Google Workspace (G):** MX `smtp.google.com` priority 1, SPF `v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com ~all`, DKIM key from the Admin console, verification TXT.

**For any setup, add DMARC:** TXT name `_dmarc`, value `v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:sales@yourdomain.in` (start with `p=none`, move to `quarantine` after a month of clean reports).

## 8. Step by step for the owner
1. **Pick the registrar** that sells `.in` (Hostinger India, GoDaddy India, BigRock or Namecheap) and search `isapurindustrialpark.in`. Check the total with tax and the renewal price. Complete e-KYC if asked. Buy `.in`, then `.com`.
2. **Use your own business email** for the registrant account, turn on two-factor login, and save the recovery codes. Never share the password in chat.
3. **Move DNS to Cloudflare** (free): add the site in Cloudflare, copy its two nameservers, paste them at the registrar. Wait up to a few hours.
4. **Set up email:** create a Zoho Mail free account, add the domain, copy the verification TXT, MX, SPF and DKIM into Cloudflare DNS (set the records to "DNS only"). Create `sales@` and add `info@` as an alias.
5. **Add DMARC** (section 7).
6. **Tell me when done.** I will then: set the domain in the site config and canonical URLs, create the Cloudflare Pages project settings, update the NAP file, put the website link with `utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp` into the Google Business Profile plan, and replace placeholder contact emails.
7. **Test:** send a mail to `sales@` from another account and reply from it. Check it lands in the inbox of a Gmail address (not spam).
