Site and competitors

The road, the rivals and the empty space between them.

IIP Phase 1 sits on the Hapur-Modinagar Road at Yusufpur Isapur. For traffic arriving from the Bhojpur side it is the first park on the road, ahead of SHD and CKS, which are both within 700 metres.

Locality

Satellite view

What the land is

Flat, intensely farmed land: green and tan field patchwork, tree lines, a handful of villages (Isapur, Bhadola, Kilhauda, Nahali). From the road, boards are seen against foliage, concrete and hazy sky. This is why neither green nor sky blue will work for IIP.

How people arrive

Modinagar connects to the Delhi-Meerut Expressway through the Bhojpur exit, which is on the northwest side of IIP. Most visitors from Delhi and Ghaziabad will pass the IIP site first, then the competitor cluster.

Nearby on the Maps listing

Pragati Automobile Agriculture Implements, Namaste India plant, Kirpa Ram Dairy, Bhojpur Public School, and a roadside liquor shop near CKS. A mix of agricultural and light industrial neighbours.

What nobody uses

  • Generous empty space
  • Charcoal or near-black boards
  • A thin frame and one rule instead of stamps and badges
  • A symbol other than a monogram or a house icon
  • Boards with only the name and phone numbers
Pattern across every competitor: a rendered gate or township image, a price or approval badge, two phone numbers, navy, gold or red. Copy density is high, so on a pole at 40 km/h only the phone numbers are readable. That is the gap: IIP can be recognised from far away because it carries almost no copy.
