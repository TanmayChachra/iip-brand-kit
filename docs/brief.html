Brand brief, version 1.0, 2026-11-07

ISAPUR Industrial Park

A short brief of who we are, how we look and sound, where the brand shows up, and what we still need from you. Full rules are in the brand kit and the brand book PDF.

1

The brand on one page

Idea

A modern industrial address on the Hapur-Modinagar Road for local manufacturers: clear plots, real facilities, one number to call.

Taglines (adaptive, pick one per creative)

A Modern Industrial Address.

The Groundwork for Growth.

Type

Montserrat for headlines and signage, Inter for body. Free fonts.

Rules

  • The logo is never changed.
  • English only. Maximum 7 words on a board.
  • No claim without a document on file.

Colour

White#FFFFFFPrimary board and page background
Charcoal#111827Text, contrast, wordmark
Yellow#FFD600Highlight, stripe, CTA (the badge artwork keeps its own #FEF102)
Red#E53935Accent line and highlight only
Green#0F4D3ASecondary: stripe, sustainability badge
Mist#F2F4F6Surfaces, panels, subtle sunburst

Next phase: social and online presence

  1. Week 1. Confirm handles, set profile picture (badge), cover and bio on Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube, WhatsApp Business, Google Business Profile.
  2. Week 1 to 2. Site photo shoot (gate, 50 ft road, lights, greenery, drainage, plots, drone).
  3. Week 2 onward. Three posts a week: one fact, one proof, one action. Status and story daily for WhatsApp.
  4. Month 1. One page website with map, distances (stating straight-line or driven), plot list, documents and a call button.

Proposed plan. Starter templates are in the brand kit under Social media.
