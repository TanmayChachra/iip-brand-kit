Decide

Quiet by day, Ink by night.

Both are minimal. Quiet is the safer daytime board. Ink is the one that stands out most and works lit. Our suggestion: Ink as the main pole board, Quiet for the hoarding and paper.

Rollout

Five phases

Phase 0 · Decide (week 1 to 2)

Board picks a direction and the size question (below). Fix the phone number, address line and which claims may appear. Get a vector master of the logo.

Phase 1 · The road (week 3 to 6)

Pole boards along the Hapur-Modinagar corridor from the Bhojpur side to past the competitor cluster. Every pole within sight of a competitor stack gets one. Cost driver: number of poles.

Phase 2 · The landmark (week 5 to 10)

Two highway hoardings (the Bhojpur approach and the Modinagar approach) and the gate fascia at the park entrance. Cost drivers: monthly site rent, structure, printing.

Phase 3 · The paper and the phone (week 6 to 8)

Visiting cards, letterhead, one-page brochure, WhatsApp status pack, site-visit message. Almost free once the kit is approved.

Phase 4 · Measure (week 8 onward)

A single call-back number or WhatsApp number per board family, and a tally of enquiries by source. Only then decide whether to scale or switch the creative.
