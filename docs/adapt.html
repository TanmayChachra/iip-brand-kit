How to adapt

Change one thing, keep the brand.

A brand kit is useful only if anyone can extend it correctly. This is how this one is structured, and what we found in the research.

The three layers

What lives where

1 · Tokens

Colours, fonts, clear space, minimum sizes. One file. Changing a colour here changes every asset. Studios call this a single source of truth.

2 · Content

Words, phone numbers, address, tagline, proof lines on or off. One file. A new campaign line is one edit.

3 · Templates

The layouts. Pole board, hoarding, card, letterhead, status. They read layers 1 and 2, so they cannot drift out of brand.

Never change

  • The badge: no recolour, stretch, rotate, effects.
  • Clear space and minimum size.
  • The wordmark proportions.
  • More than 7 words on a board.

Safe to change

  • Tagline, phone numbers, address.
  • Photo in the photo slot (hero-photo).
  • Proof lines on or off.
  • Which template and size you use.

Research summary

What the sources say, and what we did

Structure

  • Order sections from most recognisable to most nuanced: logo, colour, type, graphic elements, templates (4over4).
  • Three token layers (primitive, semantic, component), and every value defined in exactly one file (Brandy HQ). We use two files, tokens and content, because the kit is small.
  • Write it so a freelancer who has never met the brand can produce on-brand work on the first brief.

Files

  • Master vector, then export the rest. AI is the editable master, EPS or PDF for print, SVG for web, PNG as fallback (BrandCloud, JUST Creative). We use SVG as the editable master because it opens everywhere and is free.
  • Converting a raster to vector does not make a true vector logo. The badge here is a trace, so find the original.
  • CMYK for print, RGB for screens. Confirm CorelDRAW needs with your printer: export PDF or EPS from the SVG.

Hoardings and poles

  • Common Indian hoarding sizes: 20 x 10 ft medium, 40 x 20 ft large (SmartAds). We drew 40 x 20 and 40 x 15.
  • Seven or fewer words, simple sans-serif, high contrast, leave generous empty space (MacMyths, Porlier).
  • Letter height: rules range from 1 in per 10 ft to 1 in per 30 ft of distance. We assume the cautious end for the 40 x 20 hoarding. Check at full scale from the approach lane.

What you do next

  1. Find the original vector logo, or approve a redraw.
  2. Decide proof lines on or off, and match wording to documents.
  3. Replace the AI warehouse photo with a real site photo when possible.
  4. Ask the printer for exact flex size, bleed and file type.
  5. Print a small colour proof of yellow, red and green before the full run.
