Routes

Eight territories, two chosen.

Each moodboard was a different answer to the same road. They are mood only, not final artwork: the logo may drift slightly in the generated boards.

kept

1 · Quiet paper

Maximum white space. One red rule. The calmest board on a loud road.

Chosen: becomes the Quiet kit.

dropped

2 · Portal doorway

The badge inside a vermilion arch. One ownable shape.

Dropped: the red door was not used thoughtfully and read as a bad omen.

kept

3 · Ink night

The only dark board. Cream type, one yellow power line.

Chosen: becomes the Ink kit.

parked

4 · Institutional serif

DLF-style trust. Stone, brass, serif.

Parked: serif and brass is premium but adds colours and weight.

parked

5 · Monochrome grid

Karyan-style black and white. The yellow badge is the only colour.

Parked: black and white grid. Karyan-style.

parked

6 · One colour owned

Vodafone-style. Recognisable from far away by colour alone.

Parked: Vodafone-style single colour. The lesson (recognised from afar) is already in Quiet and Ink.

parked

7 · Blueprint plot grid

The park as a planned grid. Technical, honest, ready to build.

Parked: planned-grid idea, but busy.

route

8 · Editorial concept

Your conceptual adverts as a separate premium layer.

Kept separate as the Concept route.

Inspiration

What you shared

Real-estate adverts, Vodafone and Nescafe colour ownership, Emirates and Lufthansa restraint, Karyan, DLF and SG as developer benchmarks. Useful patterns: one colour owned, one clear idea, generous empty space.
