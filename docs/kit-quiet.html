Direction

Quiet

Let the badge breathe. Warm white board, thin black frame, the full-colour badge, one red rule. The calmest board on the road.

1 · Idea

Why it works here

Every competitor board is crowded: price, stamps, rendered gates, two phone numbers. Quiet does the opposite. About 70 percent of the board is empty on purpose. That gives the viewer breathing space and makes it the clearest thing on the pole, while the badge's yellow and red carry all the colour.

Risk: White boards show dust first. The black frame stops the board dissolving into hazy sky.
2 · Logo

The logo, unchanged

Primary. The full-colour badge, as supplied. No vector exists yet, so print only after the original is found.

Clear space. At least one tenth of the badge width on every side.

Minimum size. 24 px on screen, 18 mm in print. On boards never below 30 percent of the board width.

Never stretch, recolour, rotate, outline or add shadow to the badge.
