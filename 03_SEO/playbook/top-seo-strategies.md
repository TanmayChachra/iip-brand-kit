# Top SEO strategies, turned into an IIP playbook

Written 2026-11-08. Scope: a brand new Isapur Industrial Park website with only a Google Business Profile (GBP) today.

**Verdict:** For a new local industrial park the fastest wins are (1) a complete, reviewed GBP, (2) a fast, indexable site with proof pages (approvals, roads, power, distances) and (3) third-party mentions that tie the name "Isapur Industrial Park" to "industrial plots near Modinagar, Hapur and the Delhi-Meerut Expressway".
**Evidence:** Google's own docs and Whitespark's expert survey agree that profile completeness, reviews and relevance lead local results. Ahrefs, Backlinko and Sparktoro all say mentions and brand recognition now matter as much as raw traffic.
**Caveat:** Most expert pages are paywalled or blocked to automated reading. Items marked R were read only through search summaries. No source found covers industrial parks specifically, so the order below is judgement (G) built on those sources.

## Truth labels
- **V** = verified: I opened the page and read it (checked 2026-11-08).
- **R** = reported: only a search summary or a third party write up was read. Open the link before relying on a number.
- **G** = guess: my judgement for IIP, not stated by a source.

## How scoring works
Ease and Impact are each 1 to 5. Score = Ease x Impact (max 25). Effort is a rough number of hours for one person. Timeline is when results can reasonably show.

## Things the top people agree on
1. Basics still win: indexed, fast, helpful, trusted (Google docs, V).
2. Reviews and a complete GBP lead local rankings (Google V, Whitespark R, Sterling Sky R).
3. Be mentioned by others in the right category. "Ahrefs is an SEO platform" style repetition matters, not "a popular tool" (Ahrefs V).
4. Zero click is real: about 68% of US Google searches ended without a click in early 2026 per SparkToro via Similarweb (R, US data only). Branded, local and high intent searches still pay off (R).
5. Do not mass produce pages (Google V).
6. Neil Patel: "search everywhere optimization" and structured, direct answers (R, his own page was teaser only). [Neil Patel SEO 101](https://neilpatel.com/blog/how-to-think-about-seo/).
