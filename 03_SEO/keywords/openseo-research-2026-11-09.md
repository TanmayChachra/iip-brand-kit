# OpenSEO research run, 2026-11-09 (project: Isapur Industrial Park)

**Verdict:** Our Google Maps position is already strong and the organic field is almost empty. Phase 1 ranks first for "industrial plots" from the park and top 3 across a 12 km area. The organic web has little measurable demand or competition: the competitors' sites show no organic traffic data at all. So: protect and grow the Maps position first, then build a small, correct site and trust content.
**Evidence:** OpenSEO project `46ae0e81-28ff-4b06-ae7f-aa188879b44c` (https://openseo.tanmay-hq.in/p/46ae0e81-28ff-4b06-ae7f-aa188879b44c), data from DataForSEO. Run cost about 0.42 USD, balance now about 50.56 USD.
**Caveat:** English only plus two Hindi seeds, Google Ads based volumes (close variants grouped). Hindi seeds returned mostly unrelated Hindi queries. Local intent in Hindi and Hinglish is still unmeasured.

## 1. Local Maps (the strongest finding)
- Local SERP for "industrial plots" from the park pin: 1 Isapur Phase 1 (4.5 stars, 11 reviews), 2 SHD Industrial Park (4.7, 13), 3 CKS Industrial park (5.0, 40), 4 Isapur Phase 2 (5.0, 3), 5 Isapur Phase 3 (no rating), 6 CKS (UPSIDA approved profile), 7 Mahamaya (5.0, 6), 8 Sri Ratan Industrial Complex, 9 Shivaay Industrial Park.
- 5x5 rank grid, keyword "industrial plots", 3 km spacing (about 12 km square around the park): Phase 1 averages rank 1.6, is first at 15 of 25 points and top 3 at all 25. SHD takes the east side, Teja Industrial Park the south west corner.
- Phase 1, 2 and 3 together take 3 of the top 5 places near the park. Three separate profiles help here, so keep them separate (owner decision).
- CKS profile (read): description pasted from a template, only 22 photos, 5.0 stars on 40 reviews. Its edge is review count and a description. Ours is distance, three profiles and a #1 base.

## 3. Competitors online
- DataForSEO has no organic traffic or ranked-keyword data for cksindustrialpark.com or shdindustrialpark.co.in: they are below its threshold. No SERP competitor domains returned for our 10 keywords either.
- CKS backlinks: 20 backlinks from 12 referring domains, mostly low quality (spam score up to 52, two look like agencies). Any real local links we earn beat that.

## 4. What this changes in the plan
1. Maps first: finish the three profiles (descriptions, services, hours, website link once the domain is live, 20 new reviews on Phase 1, photos). This is where visits and calls come from.
2. Site: one strong home page and plots page for "industrial plots in Ghaziabad" and "industrial plot for sale", an "industrial areas in Ghaziabad" guide page for the 1,300 searches, and the location landing pages as supporting content.
3. Do not spend more on volume research for exact local phrases: the demand is below the data threshold. Spend instead on rank tracking (to see movement) and a Hindi and Hinglish check.
4. Track Maps rank monthly with the same 5x5 grid (about 0.1 USD) and compare.
