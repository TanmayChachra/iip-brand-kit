## Takeaways
1. The generic head terms belong to portals. Do not fight them head on. Win the long tail and the place names (Bhojpur, Isapur, Modinagar-Hapur Road, Delhi-Meerut Expressway).
2. Government and news pages own "industrial park Modinagar". Reply with a real explainer that cites those sources (UPSIDA, GDA) and links to them. That makes IIP a reliable page about the area, not only a sales page.
3. Be listed on the portals as well (IIP gets cited there), but link back to the own site.
