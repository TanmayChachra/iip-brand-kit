# Competitor digital audit (2026-11-08)

**Verdict:** Only two real competitor sites exist from the owner's list: CKS and Shree Haridas (SHD). CKS (cksindustrialpark.com) is the one to beat. IIP has no indexed presence for its own name, so this is a clean slate.
**Evidence:** pages read directly (V) or reported by listings and search summaries (R). Raw notes: `serp-notes.md`, `competitors.json`.
**Caveat:** no keyword volumes (needs OpenSEO), no PageSpeed scores (API returned 429 without a key), competitor Google Business Profile numbers are in `05_Local-Presence/gmb/`. "Not found" does not prove a competitor has no site.

## Competitors

- Title: "CKS Industrial Park | UPSIDA Private Industrial Park in Bhojpur, Ghaziabad". H1: "Strategically Planned Industrial Spaces in Bhojpur, Ghaziabad".
- FAQPage, LocalBusiness and Organization schema, geo landing pages, 4 blog posts, WhatsApp button, canonical set, GA but no GTM, no video, about 10 images.

**Shree Haridas (SHD)**, https://shdindustrialpark.co.in (V)
