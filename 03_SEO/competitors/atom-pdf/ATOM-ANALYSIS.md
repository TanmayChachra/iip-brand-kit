# Atom Estates brochure analysis

Source: `atomestates.pdf` (5 pages, image only, no text layer), saved 2026-11-13 from atomestates.in. Read visually 2026-11-13. Every brochure claim below is **reported by the competitor (not verified)**. Nothing here is copied into any website or print file. Our own facts come only from `00_ISAPUR_KNOWLEDGE.md`.

## 1. What it is and what it sells

- Atom Estates sells "Atom Industrial Park", an industrial plotted park on the Hapur-Modinagar Road, UP. Reported by the competitor (not verified).
- Product: industrial plots, "from 300 sq m" (note: square metres, not square yards; we sell from 300 sq yd, so their entry plot is about 20 percent bigger), park of "40+ acres", "55 ft" RCC internal roads. Reported by the competitor (not verified).
- Price signals: none. No price, no payment terms, no booking amount, no phone number or address on any page. Only a website URL and a QR code on the map page. Conversion is pushed to the site.
- Approvals: a red "Govt. Approved" wax-seal graphic on the cover. No authority, number or date named. Reported by the competitor (not verified).
- Amenities listed (page 3): underground sewer and drainage, 24x7 security and surveillance, LED street lighting, power supply and substation, landscaped green belts, professional park management, "uninterrupted utility network", sewage treatment plant (STP), gated campus with security office and boom barrier (cover art).
- Connectivity (pages 4 and 5): minutes to Delhi 40, Noida 30, Ghaziabad 20, Modinagar 14, Meerut 20, Hapur 12. Map shows Modinagar 13 km and Hapur 10 km. Routes named: Modinagar-Hapur Road, Hapur Bypass/NH-9, NH-334B, Delhi-Meerut Expressway, Ganga Expressway ("nearby"), Eastern Dedicated Freight Corridor "1 km". Map places the site near "Kilhora", between Modinagar and Hapur, closer to Hapur. All reported by the competitor (not verified). Footer says distances are approximate and the map is for representation only.
- Nearby industries, plot-by-plot sizes, phases, possession, payment plans: not mentioned.
- All imagery is rendered or AI style (sunset gate, road, skyline). No real photo of the site.

## 4. Five suggestions for website and print

1. **Be specific about approval, once proof exists.** Their vague "Govt. Approved" seal is the gap. On our approvals block, name the authority (Zila Panchayat Ghaziabad) and show the stamp or map number. Until the document is on file, say nothing, as the hard rule requires.
2. **Own the contact block.** The brochure has no phone, address or hours. Put phones, address and sales hours (8 am to 8 pm, closed Tuesday) in the header or footer and on every print piece, from the single swap points.
3. **Publish measured distances with the method.** Show "straight-line" or "driving" and the date. Add one short line saying how it was measured. This beats their untimed "minutes" and is safe to defend.
4. **Lead with plot size in plain units.** From 300 sq yd, with a small converted figure beside it, and the 2000 to 2500 sq yd top end for larger factories. Do not match their larger entry size by changing ours.
5. **Draw our own connectivity map.** A dark route map with our pin at Isapur and Bhojpur, drawn originally in our palette, with only verified roads. Add the IndianOil pump at the entry and exit as a small, true local marker (no straight-line wording). Check the DFC and expressway claims independently first and add them only if the numbers hold.
