# Backlink plan beyond directories

**Verdict:** For a new local industrial site, the best links come from local news, partner and neighbour businesses, authorities, and 2 or 3 useful tools. Directories give trust and citations, not rankings.
**Evidence:** No source found confirms which big brands operate near the park, so partner links start as a to-do for the owner (see Partners below). HARO-style tools exist, but their status and prices changed in 2025 and 2026.
**Caveat:** Effort scale: S = under 2 hours, M = a day, L = a week or more. Nothing here is guaranteed. Link value is a guess until measured in Search Console and the OpenSEO data.

## Rules before any outreach

1. Website live, with a real About, Contact, plot enquiry form and no fake claims.
2. Developer name stays hidden. Pitch under the park name.
3. Every claim in a pitch has proof on file (knowledge file section 3).
4. Never pay for links. Never use link farms. Disclose affiliation on forums.

## Partners block (needs owner input)

- The owner named Amul, Inox Air and Patanjali as brands already set up in the region. A 2026-11-08 search ([Modinagar region search](https://www.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modinagar)) found no source placing them in Modinagar or on the expressway. Label: **reported by owner, not verified**.
- Do before naming them: confirm each plant location (address and distance from the park), get written OK or use only public facts like "nearby industries include...", and avoid logos unless a licence is given.
- Petrol pump at the park exit: owner stated. Need: brand name of the pump (or independent), photo, Maps pin. Fact stays "at the exit of the park".

## Free query platforms (journalists)

- [Source of Sources](https://sourceofsources.com/) free, email queries (reported).
- [Qwoted](https://www.qwoted.com/) free tier limited to 3 pitches a month (reported).
- [SourceBottle](https://www.sourcebottle.com/) free, strong outside the US (reported).
- [Help a B2B Writer](https://www.helpab2bwriter.com/) free to pitch (reported).
- [Featured.com](https://featured.com/) paid since April 2025 (reported). Skip for now.
- Source: [HARO alternatives roundup](https://searchatlas.com/blog/haro-alternatives/) (vendor blog, checked 2026-11-08). HARO status claims conflict, treat as unverified.

## Tracking

- Google Search Console links report (free), plus OpenSEO once hosted.
- Log every outreach in `03_SEO/backlinks/outreach-log.csv` (date, target, pitch, result, link URL). Create the file when the first pitch goes out.
- Do not chase quantity. Target 1 good local link per week for 12 weeks.
