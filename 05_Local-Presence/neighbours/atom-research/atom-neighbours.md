**Verdict:** The Atom (CKS Atom) brochure PDF could not be found. No page I could reach lists Atom's "nearby companies". The only neighbour names in the open are the ones the owner gave (Inox Air, Namaste India) and one competitor mention (SHD: "Near Inox oxygen centre").

## What was tried
- Web searches: "Atom Industrial Park brochure pdf", "CKS Atom industrial park pdf", "Atom Industrial Park nearby Inox Namaste India", "industrial park Modinagar nearby industries", plus brochure and layout PDF searches on UPSIDA, UP MSME Connect and RERA style portals. No Atom brochure came up. The closest PDFs were for other parks (Umesh Modi brochure, New Hapur Industrial Pledge Park on UP MSME Connect), and none mention Atom.
- Guessed official domains (atomindustrialpark.com, .in, cksatom.com) did not respond.
- Atom has no website of its own that I could find. It appears on broker portals only. A NoBroker listing "Plot/Land for Sale in CKS Atom Industrial Park Modinagar" (https://www.nobroker.in/property/plot/buy/plot-for-sale-in-modinagar-ghaziabad/8a9f92038f70567a018f70a258da29b2/detail , reported) describes a gated park with sewerage, RCC roads, CCTV and 24x7 security, and lists no neighbouring companies.
- CKS Industrial Park's `llms.txt` (https://cksindustrialpark.com/llms.txt , read directly): Village Kilhora, Bhojpur, Tehsil Modinagar. It lists connectivity (expressway, Eastern Peripheral Expressway, RRTS) but no neighbour companies.
- SHD site (read earlier, see `03_SEO/competitors/findings.md`): "Near Inox oxygen centre". It is the only competitor naming a neighbouring brand.
- Two attempts to read search engine result pages by command line were blocked by the safety classifier, so I stopped trying to scrape search engines.
