# Neighbour brand verification (2026-11-08)

**Rule:** we name a brand on the IIP site only if it is VERIFIED nearby AND the owner agrees. Brand names are used as plain text only. Logos are trademarked: do not use them without written permission from the brand. Even a true neighbour fact can mislead if worded like an endorsement, so wording must be factual ("INOX Air Products operates an air separation plant in Modinagar") and never imply partnership.

Labels: VERIFIED (read at a primary or authoritative source), REPORTED (third party, directory or competitor), UNVERIFIED (no source found).
