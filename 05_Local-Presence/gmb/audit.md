# Google Business Profile audit: Isapur Industrial Park

Audited 2026-11-08 from public Google Maps in a signed-out browser (limited view). Nothing was edited, posted or answered.

## Verdict (3 lines)
1. Three separate profiles exist (Phase 1, Phase 2, Phase 3) plus two unclaimed duplicate "Building" pins. Reviews, photos and authority are split four ways, and none has a website, a description or a current review.
2. Phase 1 (4.5 stars, 11 reviews, 27 photos) is the strongest asset. It ranks 4th for "industrial park Modinagar" in the limited view, but the park does not appear at all for "industrial plots in Modinagar".
3. Biggest wins are cheap: add the website link with UTM, fix the phone numbers (Phase 3 shows a number that is not on the brand list), write a description, add services, ask for 20 fresh reviews, post weekly.

Truth labels used: VERIFIED (seen on the page, see screenshots), REPORTED (third party), GUESS.
